Land cleanup

The right tool for your ground.

Our skid steer runs two attachments. Here is what each one does, so you can tell us what your job needs.

Rake grapple

Long tines with a clamp on top. It grabs brush, branches and debris and lets the dirt fall through, so your topsoil stays where it is.

Best for

  • Overgrown lots and brush
  • Fallen limbs and small trees
  • Debris piles and old fencing
  • Loading junk straight into the trailer

Bucket

A smooth-edged scoop for moving material. It digs, carries, spreads and levels, and leaves a clean, even surface.

Best for

  • Moving dirt, gravel and mulch
  • Light grading and levelling
  • Scraping and backfill
  • Clearing loose rubble
